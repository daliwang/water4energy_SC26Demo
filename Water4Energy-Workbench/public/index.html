What decisions does water information support?

Store water or retain flood space?
Spring refill depends on expected rain and inflow, while reservoirs also need room for high water.
Maintain downstream water
Storage and releases support water supply, navigation, water quality and aquatic habitat.
Plan water for energy
Water availability affects hydropower opportunities and the cooling water needed by thermal plants.
Preserve cold-water resources
The TVA briefing connects spring storage at Norris to summer thermal concerns downstream.
